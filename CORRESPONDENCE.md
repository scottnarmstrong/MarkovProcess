# Correspondence: textbook results ↔ Lean

This library is not the formalization of a single text. This document maps the standard
results of the theory of Feller processes, as found in the references listed in
[`formalization.yaml`](formalization.yaml), to the Lean declarations that deliver them, so that
a reader who knows the textbooks can locate each result. The consumer guide
[`docs/CONSUMER_GUIDE.md`](docs/CONSUMER_GUIDE.md) is the finer-grained, usage-oriented map.

**Conventions.** Lean names are relative to the `MarkovProcess` namespace; file paths are
relative to the repository root. **Status**: `proved`, formalized under the conventions of
[`docs/CONVENTIONS.md`](docs/CONVENTIONS.md) (`NNReal` times, Mathlib's stopping times and
stopped sigma-algebra, raw canonical filtration, every starting point);
`comparator-checked`, additionally certified by `leanprover/comparator` against a Mathlib-only
statement (`Audit/`); `partial`, formalized in a restricted form stated in the note;
`not-formalized`, absent.
